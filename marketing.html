Store description

A little twist can unlock the whole puzzle.

Ring Bonbon is a colorful 3D puzzle game about interlocked rings. Rotate each ring to line up its opening and release it from the puzzle. When a ring is blocked, inspect the shape, try another angle, and choose your next move.

Use a two-finger gesture to rotate the whole puzzle and see how the rings fit together. Look closely, think ahead, and enjoy finding a path through each challenge.

Features:
- Colorful 3D ring puzzles
- Rotate individual rings to align their openings
- Turn the whole puzzle to inspect it from different sides
- Earn in-game coins as you play
- Adjustable sound and vibration
- No account required
- Progress and preferences saved on your device

Take a closer look, find the opening, and let the rings go.

Store review notes draft

  • Account: no registration or login is required.
  • Gameplay: rotate individual rings to align their openings; use two fingers to rotate the entire puzzle.
  • Local saves: progress, coins, and preferences are stored on the device. No developer-operated cloud save or progress transfer is currently provided.
  • Purchases: the currently documented version has no in-app purchases. Coins are gameplay rewards. Confirm this against the submitted build.
  • Advertising: planned AppLovin MAX mediation includes AppLovin, Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff), Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), BidMachine, and Bigo Ads, with Google AdMob and DT Exchange also planned on iOS. List the actual enabled networks and ad formats for the review build.
  • Other SDKs: Firebase, Meta (Facebook), and AppsFlyer integrations are being added. Specify the enabled Firebase products and measured events for the submitted build.
  • Tracking: on iOS, obtain the required App Tracking Transparency authorization before tracking or accessing the IDFA. Verify the consent flow in the final build; these draft notes do not certify SDK behavior.
  • Connectivity: local saves are separate from online services. Enabled ads, analytics, diagnostics, attribution, and remote configuration may require network access.

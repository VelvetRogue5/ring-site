This policy explains how Ring Bonbon (app identifier ai.ringbonbon.game) handles information. You can play without creating an account. Your game progress and preferences are saved on your device.

No ad tracking

Information handled by the game

Ring Bonbon stores gameplay data locally to remember your unlocked level, in-game coin balance, and sound and vibration settings. Existing local cosmetic-unlock preferences may also be read if present. This data is used to provide game features and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. It does not require an account or ask for payment card details.

Advertising, analytics, and third-party services

The current version does not integrate advertising networks, install-attribution SDKs, remote configuration, cloud saves, or a gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service. Unity advertising, analytics, and cloud diagnostics are disabled in the current project configuration. The game does not use the advertising identifier for tracking.

The game is built with Unity. The presence of the game engine does not mean Unity advertising or analytics is enabled. App stores and operating systems may independently process download information, purchases made outside this game, backups, or diagnostics under their own privacy settings and policies; that processing is separate from the local game saves described here.

In-app purchases

The current version does not offer in-app purchases. Coins are in-game rewards, not real currency, and cannot currently be purchased through the game.

Support and this website

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you send. We use that information to respond to your request and troubleshoot problems. Please do not include sensitive information unless necessary.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your email provider handles messages under its own privacy policy.

Sharing and use of information

We do not sell or rent player personal information. The current game does not send local saves to advertisers or analytics providers. Information you voluntarily provide through support is used to handle your request and may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect safety, security, or legal rights.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until replaced through gameplay or removed with the app's data. On Android, you can clear app storage in system settings. On iOS, deleting the app normally removes its local data; offloading is different and can retain data. Operating-system backups may retain or restore data according to your backup settings. The game does not provide its own cloud-save service.

Removing local data may permanently erase progress and coins. Sound and vibration can be changed in the game's Settings. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain support records, and meet legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent us. We cannot remotely access or erase game saves on your device.

Security

Local saves use the device's app storage. We do not claim that any storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and operating system up to date and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

Children's privacy

The current game has no account registration, chat, or advertising and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, please contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current version of Ring Bonbon. We will revise it and update the date when relevant practices change. If a future release adds advertising, analytics, purchases, accounts, cloud saves, or another data service, its privacy disclosures must be updated before that feature is released.

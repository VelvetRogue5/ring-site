This policy explains how Ring Bonbon (app identifier ai.ringbonbon.game) handles information. You can play without creating an account. Your game progress and preferences are saved on your device.

Ads and measurement

Releases with advertising, Firebase, Meta (Facebook), or AppsFlyer may process device identifiers, app activity, and diagnostics, depending on enabled features and your privacy choices.

Information handled by the game

Ring Bonbon stores gameplay data locally to remember your unlocked level, in-game coin balance, and sound and vibration settings. Existing local cosmetic-unlock preferences may also be read if present. This data is used to provide game features and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. It does not require an account or ask for payment card details.

Advertising, analytics, and third-party services

We are introducing advertising, Firebase, the Meta (Facebook) SDK, and AppsFlyer. This policy covers releases that enable these integrations. The information processed depends on the services and SDK features enabled in your installed version, your region, device settings, and consent choices.

These services may receive IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device model, operating system and app version, language, app or installation identifiers, advertising identifiers where permitted, session and gameplay events, install and referral information, ad impressions and clicks, and crash or performance diagnostics. Local game saves are separate from these events; keeping progress on your device does not mean all app activity stays on your device.

  • Firebase / Google: enabled features may provide app usage analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, or remote configuration. Analytics may process app events and identifiers; diagnostic features may process crash logs, device details, and installation identifiers. See Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising partners: advertising SDKs and any mediation partners may process device and ad interaction information to deliver ads, limit repeated ads, measure results, and prevent fraud. Where permitted and with required consent, this information may also support personalized advertising. The partners involved depend on the advertising integration in the release.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK: enabled app-event and measurement features may send app launches, events, device identifiers, and technical information to Meta to measure campaigns and support advertising. Meta may associate this information with other information it holds, subject to applicable settings and consent. A Facebook account or Facebook login is not required to play. See the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • AppsFlyer (AF): attribution and marketing analytics may process installation and referral data, app events, IP addresses, device information, and permitted identifiers to measure how players discover and use the app and to detect fraud. Attribution or event information may be shared with configured campaign partners. See the AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

Ring Bonbon is built with Unity. App stores and operating systems may independently process downloads, backups, or diagnostics under their own policies and settings.

Consent and advertising choices

Where required, we request consent before enabling processing that requires it. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier and tracking across other companies' apps or websites require authorization through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change that permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, advertising ID and ad privacy controls are available in system settings; options vary by device and operating-system version.

You may also use any privacy or consent controls presented in the app. Refusing tracking permission does not necessarily stop all analytics, diagnostics, attribution, or contextual ads: processing that is permitted without that permission may continue. Contact us if you need help exercising your choices.

In-app purchases

The current version does not offer in-app purchases. Coins are in-game rewards, not real currency, and cannot currently be purchased through the game.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Sharing and use of information

We use information to operate and improve the game, diagnose problems, deliver and measure ads, understand campaign performance, prevent fraud, and answer support requests. We share relevant information with the service providers and advertising or measurement partners described above. Depending on the applicable law, sharing identifiers and app activity for cross-context or targeted advertising may be considered a sale, sharing, or targeted advertising even when no money is exchanged for the data. Applicable consent and opt-out rights apply. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect safety, security, or legal rights. Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until replaced through gameplay or removed with the app's data. On Android, you can clear app storage in system settings. On iOS, deleting the app normally removes its local data; offloading is different and can retain data. Operating-system backups may retain or restore data according to your backup settings. The game does not provide its own cloud-save service.

Removing local data may permanently erase progress and coins. Sound and vibration can be changed in the game's Settings. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain support records, and meet legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent us. We cannot remotely access or erase game saves on your device. SDK data retention varies by provider, data type, and configuration; deleting the app does not automatically delete information already transmitted. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing, including withdrawal of consent and opt-out of targeted advertising or sharing. Email us to make a request; we may need information to verify and locate the relevant records, and legal exceptions may apply.

Security

Local saves use the device's app storage. We do not claim that any storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device and operating system up to date and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age at which parental consent is required under applicable law without that consent. Advertising and measurement integrations must use age-appropriate restrictions where required; personalized advertising and tracking must not be enabled for children where prohibited. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, an integrated service, or support, contact us so we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion.

Ring Bonbon

A colorful 3D ring puzzle.

How to play

Rotate individual rings to line up their openings and release them from the puzzle. Interlocked rings can block one another, so try a different ring or angle when a move is blocked. Use a two-finger gesture to rotate the whole puzzle and inspect it from another side.

Sound and vibration

Open the game's Settings to change sound and vibration. If sound is enabled but you cannot hear it, check your device volume, silent mode, and connected headphones.

Progress and coins

Level progress, coins, and preferences are saved locally. The current game has no account or cloud-save service and does not support transferring progress between devices. Deleting the app or clearing its storage may erase your progress. Please contact us before reinstalling to troubleshoot a problem.

Report a technical issue

Email velvet_rogue_5@proton.me with your device model, operating system version, game version, level number, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot or short recording can help. Remove personal information from attachments before sending them. Do not send passwords or payment details.

If the app stops responding, close and reopen it. Check for an available game update. If the issue continues, contact us with the details above. We cannot access your device's local saves remotely.

Advertising issues and rewards

In releases with advertising, ads are served through AppLovin MAX and its advertising partners. If an ad does not load, check your internet connection and try again later; an ad may not be available for your device or region at that time. Close and reopen the game if an ad leaves it unresponsive.

If an optional rewarded ad is offered, follow the completion instructions shown in the game. If a reward is missing, email support with the game version, level, approximate time and time zone, and what happened. Include the advertiser or a screenshot if available, with personal information removed. Please report inappropriate ads with the same details. We cannot guarantee recovery of a missed reward; avoid reinstalling or clearing storage while we investigate.

Tracking and privacy choices

On iOS, you can change tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See Apple's tracking permission guide. On Android, look for advertising ID and ad privacy controls in your system settings; options vary by device and version. See Google's settings guide. Use any privacy or consent controls presented in the game, or contact us for help.

Turning off tracking does not necessarily remove ads or stop all permitted analytics and diagnostics. Local saves do not prevent enabled advertising, Firebase, Meta (Facebook), or AppsFlyer services from processing device and usage information. See our Privacy Policy for the data and services involved.

Purchases and unavailable modes

The current version has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned in gameplay. Shop and additional game modes are not available in the current release.

Privacy requests

For questions about information handling, consent choices, or requests to access, correct, or delete information held by us or our integrated services, contact the same support address. Tell us which app and platform your request concerns; we may ask for additional information to locate relevant records. Do not send passwords or payment details. Deleting the app does not automatically delete information already transmitted to service providers. We cannot remotely erase local saves. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Ring Bonbon

A colorful 3D ring puzzle.

How to play

Rotate individual rings to line up their openings and release them from the puzzle. Interlocked rings can block one another, so try a different ring or angle when a move is blocked. Use a two-finger gesture to rotate the whole puzzle and inspect it from another side.

Sound and vibration

Open the game's Settings to change sound and vibration. If sound is enabled but you cannot hear it, check your device volume, silent mode, and connected headphones.

Progress and coins

Level progress, coins, and preferences are saved locally. The current game has no account or cloud-save service and does not support transferring progress between devices. Deleting the app or clearing its storage may erase your progress. Please contact us before reinstalling to troubleshoot a problem.

Report a technical issue

Email velvet_rogue_5@proton.me with your device model, operating system version, game version, level number, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot or short recording can help. Remove personal information from attachments before sending them. Do not send passwords or payment details.

If the app stops responding, close and reopen it. Check for an available game update. If the issue continues, contact us with the details above. We cannot access your device's local saves remotely.

Purchases and unavailable modes

The current version has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned in gameplay. Shop and additional game modes are not available in the current release.

Privacy requests

For questions about information handling or deletion of information you sent through email, contact the same support address. See our Privacy Policy for details.
